TrackTrack

Know your stock. Know who owes you.

Inventory, purchasing and credit for wholesalers — built for the doorway, not the office.

  • Counted in cartons. Stock, prices and orders in the unit you actually buy and sell in, with the loose bottles kept.
  • Purchase to shelf. Raise an order, receive it partly or in full, and see which supplier is really cheapest.
  • Every cedi owed. Sell on credit, age the debt, and record payments against the sale they belong to.
  • Works without network. Keep selling when the signal drops; it syncs when it comes back.
