TrackTrack demo - voiceover

Read unhurried. The captions in the video are already timed to these lines, so
a steady read lands close without much nudging. The full cut runs about three
and a quarter minutes; the short cut is the four starred beats.

Cast a Ghanaian voice. A British or American read on a product built for
Spintex Road lands as an import, and the whole pitch is that this one is not.

---

Every morning, TrackTrack opens on what needs you.
Not a menu. A list that empties itself.

*  Say you are selling two cartons of Vitamalt.
*  Find it by name.
*  Two. Not forty-eight.
*  It is already counting in cartons, because that is how you sell.
*  Who bought it, and how they paid.
*  This one goes on credit.
*  And the invoice says two cartons.
*  Forty-eight bottles left the shelf on their own.

*  The money still out there, oldest debt first.
*  By name, by how old it is, and what they paid last.

When you restock, it remembers what you paid before.
And who you paid it to, so you can see who is cheapest.

Goods arrive. You receive them line by line.
Part of an order today, the rest next week. Stock only moves here.

Stock counted the way you buy it.
Cartons, and the loose bottles that are really on the floor.

*  And when the network goes.
*  You keep selling. It queues, and syncs when the signal comes back.

What sold, what it cost you, what is on the shelf.
Every report says which unit its numbers are in.

It starts free. Twenty products, one person.
Grow, and the plan grows with you. Nothing renews on its own.

*  TrackTrack. Know your stock, know who owes you.
