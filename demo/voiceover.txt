TrackTrack demo - voiceover

Read unhurried. About sixty seconds at a natural pace. The captions in the
video are already timed to these lines, so a steady read lands close without
much nudging.

Cast a Ghanaian voice. A British or American read on a product built for
Spintex Road lands as an import, and the whole pitch is that this one is not.

---

Every morning, TrackTrack opens on what needs you.

Not a menu. A list that empties itself.

You sell by the carton, so it counts by the carton.

Two cartons - and the shelf drops by forty-eight bottles on its own.

And the money still out there. By name, by how old it is, and what they
paid last.

When you restock, it remembers what you paid before. And who you paid it to.

Stock counted the way you buy it. Cartons, and the loose bottles.

TrackTrack. Know your stock, know who owes you.
